DM Tools

Fold&Roll is a free D&D companion app for iPhone and iPad (Android in beta) — no account, no subscription. Get Fold&Roll on the App Store

Fold&Roll Wiki — November 2026 (version 1.4)

Encounter builder

Build encounters from the 334-monster bestiary, with the math done for you. Homebrew monsters slot in beside the built-in ones (see Homebrew).

NPCs

A one-tap NPC generator for the shopkeeper your party just decided to interview. NPCs carry AC and HP on the DM's side only, and level in one tap when they somehow become regulars.

Shops that haggle back

Staged shops with real inventory and prices — and yes, they haggle. Set the shop's stock and let the party's face try their luck.

Session prep

Prep notes are free: write them, keep them with the campaign, and deploy from them at the table. A one-shot generator builds a ready-to-run session in a tap — new in 1.4, it can generate a dungeon-shaped one-shot, map included. Sending Stones and other functional magic items from the SRD work as items, not just names on a list.

Campaign Memory (Fold&Roll+)

Your whole campaign, searchable: Recall searches across session notes, the Notebook keeps the linked record, and "Previously on" surfaces the structured recap when the table sits back down. Campaign History keeps the record of past sessions alongside it.

Free limits

The free tier holds 2 campaigns. Fold&Roll+ removes the cap and unlocks Campaign History and Campaign Memory.
