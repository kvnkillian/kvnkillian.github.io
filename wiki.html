Fold&Roll Wiki

Fold&Roll is a free D&D companion app for iPhone and iPad (Android in beta) — no account, no subscription. Get Fold&Roll on the App Store

Everything about Fold&Roll, the D&D 5e companion app, in one place: what it does, how the pieces fit together, what's free and what's in the one-time Fold&Roll+ unlock. For step-by-step walkthroughs, see the DM Guide, Player Guide, and Creator Guide; this wiki is the reference layer underneath them.

Quick facts

  • What: a companion app for Dungeons & Dragons 5th edition — character sheets, spellbook, battle maps, and DM tools for the physical table and online play.
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad (live), Android (closed beta), plus an Apple Watch battle app and an Apple TV Table Display.
  • Price: free to download. Fold&Roll+ is a one-time $14.99 unlock — never a subscription.
  • Accounts: none. No sign-up, no servers holding your campaigns; the app is offline-first and everything works with no signal.
  • Rules editions: both the 2014 and 2024 rules, kept in sealed lanes — a campaign is one edition or the other, and a converter moves characters across.
  • Languages: 11 — English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Swedish, Italian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Topics

  • Getting Started — roles, your first character, joining a table, and the free built-in How to Play D&D guide.
  • Characters & Spells — sheets, the spellbook, rests, leveling, and the 2014/2024 edition lanes.
  • Battle & Maps — initiative, fog-of-war battle maps, Dice-Drop generated dungeons, and multi-floor locations.
  • DM Tools — encounter builder, NPCs, shops that haggle, session prep, and Campaign Memory.
  • Online Tables — playing in the same room ("At the Table") or remotely with Telepathic Bond, the Watch app, Live Activities, and the TV display.
  • Homebrew & Fold&Roll+ — the Homebrew Workshop, creator packs, and exactly what the free tier and the unlock each cover.
