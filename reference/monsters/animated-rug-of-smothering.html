Animated Rug of Smothering

The complete Animated Rug of Smothering stat block for D&D 5e, straight from the SRD — shown in the edition Fold&Roll carries it in.

2024 rulesAnimated Rug of Smothering

Large construct, unaligned

Armor Class 12

Hit Points 27 (5d10)

Speed 10 ft.

STR17 (+3)
DEX14 (+2)
CON10 (+0)
INT1 (-5)
WIS3 (-4)
CHA1 (-5)

Damage Immunities poison, psychic

Condition Immunities Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned

Senses blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 6

Languages None

Challenge 2 (450 XP)

Actions

Smother. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the rug can give it the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) instead of dealing damage. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The rug can smother only one creature at a time. While grappling the target, the rug can't take this action, the rug halves the damage it takes (round down), and the target takes the same amount of damage.
