Cubby: Baby Supply Tracker

Know before you run out.

Private by design — no account, no ads, no tracking: Cubby tracks the supplies you can't afford to run out of — diapers, wipes, formula, milk — learns your household's pace, and warns you before you run out.

Private by design. There is no Cubby account to create, no server holding your nursery, and nothing to sell: Cubby collects no data (the App Store lists it as “Data Not Collected”). Everything lives on your phone, or passes directly between your household's own phones over an encrypted connection. Read the privacy policy.

What Cubby does

  • Learns your pace. Cubby works out how fast your household actually uses each supply and forecasts the day you'll run out — not just how many are left.
  • The Cubby Shelf. Every supply in its own compartment: diapers by size, wipes, formula by can, and milk across freezer, deep freezer, and fridge — with per-bag, first-in-first-out tracking for the freezer stash.
  • A shopping list that writes itself. Anything running low lands on the list on its own, with pack math done for you.
  • Log the day in one tap. Feeds, sleep, and diaper changes — and logging a change takes a diaper off the shelf, so the counts stay honest.
  • Imports that do the typing. Turn a Babylist registry into your starting shelf, and let Cubby read a paper daycare day sheet into the day's log — you review every entry before anything is saved.
  • Share the shelf. Pair two phones and both parents see the same counts. One purchase covers the whole household — joining is free.
