A full solo mode for D&D 5e. Your character was ready — now the table is, too.

Pick a character from your own Fold&Roll roster and take them into a text adventure — exploring, choosing, and talking your way through the story with tap-first controls and optional typing.

When steel comes out, play moves to the same battle map you use at the table — grid, fog, tokens and all — with the app running the monsters.

Start with a free demo story. Generate a fresh delve from a seed — and share that seed so a friend plays the exact same adventure. Build your own with the in-app builder, and trade adventures as single files.

You choose at the door: play it as a tale, where what you earn carries home but the scars don't — or for real, where everything that happens to your sheet stays happened.
