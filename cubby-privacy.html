Privacy Policy for Cubby: Baby Supply Tracker

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Cubby is a baby supply tracker for iPhone, developed by Kevin Killian ("we", "us"). It counts diapers, wipes, formula, and milk, learns how fast your household uses them, and warns you before you run out. Privacy is the foundation of this app — it's built for the most personal season of your life — so this policy is short and plain: Cubby is designed so that your family's data stays with your family.

The short version

Cubby has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information — and we couldn't look at your data if we wanted to. Everything the app does happens on your device, or directly between your household's own phones.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your device. Your supplies, counts, usage history, settings, and — if you choose to enter them — your baby's name and birthdate are stored locally on your device. They stay there unless you pair a second phone for household sharing, described next.
  • Household sync between your own phones (optional). If you pair two phones for household sharing, your shelf syncs directly from phone to phone over an encrypted local connection whenever both phones have Cubby open near each other. There is no Cubby server in the middle, and nothing is stored by us.
  • No iCloud sync in this version. Cubby does not sync through iCloud at all in this version. Household sharing is phone-to-phone only, as described above. If a future version adds iCloud sync, this policy will be updated first.

Your baby's name and birthdate

Both are entirely optional, entered by you, and stored only as described above. Cubby uses them for one thing: personalizing the app — putting your child's name on their shelf, and expressing their age in words ("3 months old") on the app's recap cards and keepsakes. The full birth date never appears on any image, card, or anything else the app creates for sharing. You can change or remove the name and birthdate at any time in Settings, and the app works exactly the same without them.

Daycare sheets

If you import a daycare day sheet, you choose the photo — from your photo library, your camera, or the share sheet — and Cubby reads it entirely on your device, using on-device text recognition. The photo is never uploaded anywhere, and you review every entry before anything is saved. Cubby doesn't keep the photo: a photo shared into Cubby is deleted once you've reviewed or dismissed the import, and a photo you pick or take is read in the moment — Cubby doesn't save a copy of it.

Product links and registry import

When you paste a product link (or your public registry link) and tap to fill in an item, your device fetches that link directly so the app can read the product's name and details — the same page your browser would open. Cubby never asks for, and never receives, your registry or store account credentials, passwords, or order history. Nothing about the link is sent to us.

Purchases

The Household Unlock is sold through Apple's App Store as a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see or store your payment details. The app checks, on your device, whether the purchase is active so it can unlock — nothing about the purchase, and nothing about you, is sent to us.

Things the app never does

  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad tracking (the app never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission because it never tracks)
  • No data brokers, no sale of data to anyone, ever
  • No location collection
  • No contacts or health data access — and the only photos Cubby ever sees are the day-sheet photos you pick, take, or share into it (see Daycare sheets above)

Exporting your data

Export my data (Settings → Your data) builds one file containing your shelf, its history, your logs, and your settings right on your phone, and hands it to the share sheet — you choose where it goes: Files, AirDrop, mail, wherever you like. Cubby sends it nowhere on its own and keeps no copy. The export never contains birth dates: children appear by name and age only, the same rule as every card Cubby makes for sharing.

Deleting your data

You're in control, because the data lives with you. In Settings → Your data, Delete all Cubby data wipes everything Cubby stores on that phone. If you use household sharing, Forget this household ends the pairing on that phone and removes its household key — the other phone keeps its own copy of the shared data, so delete on each phone to remove it everywhere. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device.

Children

Cubby is a tool for parents and caregivers, not directed at children. It collects no personal information about anyone — including your baby, whose name and birthdate, if you enter them, never leave your household's own phones.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cubby changes how data works, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.
