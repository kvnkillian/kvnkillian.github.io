Help for Cubby: Baby Supply Tracker, for iPhone.

What Cubby is

Cubby keeps count of the baby supplies your household burns through — diapers by size, wipes, formula, and milk — learns how fast you use them, and warns you before you run out. You can log feeds, diaper changes, and sleep, import a daycare day sheet or your baby registry, and share the shelf with a second phone in your household. There are no accounts and no sign-up: everything lives on your device, or passes directly between your household's own phones. Cubby is free forever; a one-time Household Unlock ($9.99, no subscription) adds unlimited items, hosting a household pairing, widgets, themes, per-item alert settings, a weekly digest and monthly report, and shelves for more than one child.

Frequently asked questions

How does household sharing work?

Pair two phones once, in the app, and the shelf syncs directly from phone to phone over an encrypted connection whenever both phones have Cubby open near each other. There is no Cubby server in the middle, and nothing is stored by us. Joining a household is free; hosting the pairing is part of the Household Unlock. Cubby does not sync through iCloud in this version — sharing is phone-to-phone only, so a closed or pocketed app can't sync until it's opened near the other phone again.

I bought the Household Unlock — how do I restore it on a new phone?

The unlock is a one-time App Store purchase tied to your Apple ID, not to a device, and Family Sharing is on. Install Cubby on the new phone while signed in with the same Apple ID and open the Household Unlock screen — the app checks your purchase on the device and your unlock returns without paying again. The same works after deleting and reinstalling the app.

Does Cubby collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours — the App Store lists Cubby as "Data Not Collected," and that's the design, not just the policy. Your baby's name and birthdate are optional, stay on your household's phones, and the full birth date never appears on anything the app creates for sharing. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

How do daycare sheets and registry import work?

Both happen entirely on your device. A day-sheet photo you pick, take, or share into Cubby is read with on-device text recognition, you review every entry before anything is saved, and Cubby doesn't keep the photo. For registry import, your device fetches your public registry link directly — Cubby never asks for or receives your registry or store account credentials.

How do I delete my data?

Because the data lives on your phone, you hold the delete key: Settings → Your data → Delete all Cubby data wipes everything Cubby stores on that phone. If you use household sharing, delete on each phone to remove it everywhere, and uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device.
